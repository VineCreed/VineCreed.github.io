在中国大陆，外国人（含无国籍人）如涉嫌违法犯罪、违反出入境管理规定，或作为案件当事人、证人，可能在不同程序中面临若干种人身自由受到限制的情形。这些措施性质各异——有的是刑事强制措施，有的是行政处罚或行政强制，有的仅针对外国人。下文按程序性质分层梳理，并逐一列明法律依据与时限要点，便于对照理解。

In mainland China, a foreigner (including a stateless person) may — if suspected of an offence or of violating entry-exit rules, or as a party or witness in a case — face several kinds of measures that restrict personal liberty. These differ in nature: some are criminal compulsory measures, some are administrative penalties or administrative coercive measures, and some apply specifically to foreigners. The sections below group them by the nature of the procedure, and list the legal basis and the time limits for each.

---

## 一、刑事强制措施 · I. Criminal Compulsory Measures

在刑事侦查、审查起诉、审判阶段，为防止犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃避侦查、毁灭证据或继续犯罪，司法机关可依法对其采取限制人身自由的强制措施。以下由轻到重排列。

During criminal investigation, review for prosecution, and trial, the judicial authorities may, in accordance with law, impose measures restricting a criminal suspect's or defendant's personal liberty, to prevent flight, destruction of evidence, or further offending. They are listed below from the least to the most restrictive.

### 1. 传唤 · Summons for Questioning

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第119条。

传唤是侦查机关通知**不需要逮捕、拘留**的犯罪嫌疑人，到其所在市、县内的指定地点或到其住处接受讯问的程序，属于要求到案接受讯问、而非强制羁押。对在现场发现的犯罪嫌疑人，经出示工作证件可以口头传唤。传唤、拘传持续的时间**不得超过12小时**；案情特别重大、复杂，需要采取拘留、逮捕措施的，**不得超过24小时**。不得以连续传唤、拘传的形式变相拘禁，并应保证饮食和必要的休息时间。

**Legal basis**: Article 119 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

A summons is the procedure by which the investigating authority notifies a criminal suspect who does **not** need to be detained or arrested to appear for questioning at a designated place in the city or county where the suspect is located, or at the suspect's residence. It requires appearance for questioning rather than forcible custody. A suspect found at the scene may be summoned orally upon presentation of official identification. A summons or compulsory summons may **not exceed 12 hours**; where the case is especially serious or complex and detention or arrest is necessary, it may **not exceed 24 hours**. Consecutive summonses may not be used as a disguised form of confinement, and food and necessary rest must be provided.

### 2. 拘传 · Compulsory Summons (Compulsory Appearance)

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第119条（与传唤同条）。

拘传与传唤针对同一对象、适用同一时限，区别在于**拘传具有强制力**：经依法拘传，犯罪嫌疑人无正当理由拒绝到案的，可以被强制带到案接受讯问。二者都不是独立的羁押措施，讯问结束即应让相关人员离开，不得以连续拘传变相限制人身自由。

**Legal basis**: Article 119 of the Criminal Procedure Law (same article as the summons).

A compulsory summons concerns the same subjects and the same time limits as a summons; the difference is that a compulsory summons is **coercive**: a suspect who refuses to appear without justification may be brought to the designated place by force. Neither is an independent custodial measure — once questioning ends, the person must be released, and repeated compulsory summonses may not be used to restrict liberty in disguised form.

### 3. 刑事拘留 · Criminal Detention

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第82条。

刑事拘留是公安机关对**现行犯或者重大嫌疑分子**，在法定紧急情形下采取的临时性羁押措施，适用于：正在预备犯罪、实行犯罪或者犯罪后即时被发觉的；被被害人或在现场亲眼看见的人指认犯罪；在身边或住处发现有犯罪证据；犯罪后企图自杀、逃跑或在逃；有毁灭、伪造证据或串供可能；不讲真实姓名、住址、身份不明；有流窜作案、多次作案、结伙作案重大嫌疑等七种情形。拘留后，除无法通知或有碍侦查等法定情形外，应当**在24小时内**通知家属并讯问。公安机关一般应在拘留后**3日内**提请检察院审查批准逮捕，特殊情况可延长1–4日（合计最长7日）；流窜作案、多次作案、结伙作案的重大嫌疑分子可延长至**30日**；检察院应在收到提请后**7日内**决定是否批准逮捕（《刑事诉讼法》第91条）。

**Legal basis**: Article 82 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Criminal detention is a temporary custodial measure taken by the public security authority against a **flagrant offender or a major suspect** in legally defined urgent circumstances — e.g. a person caught in the course of or immediately after an offence; identified by the victim or an eyewitness; found with criminal evidence on his or her person or at home; attempting suicide, fleeing, or at large after an offence; likely to destroy or fabricate evidence or to collude; refusing to give a true name, address, or identity; or strongly suspected of itinerant, repeated, or gang offending. After detention, the family must be notified and the suspect questioned **within 24 hours**, except where notification is impossible or would hinder investigation. The public security authority must generally request the procuratorate to review and approve arrest **within 3 days** (extendable by 1–4 days, i.e. up to 7 days in special circumstances); for major suspects of itinerant, repeated, or gang offending, up to **30 days**. The procuratorate must decide whether to approve arrest **within 7 days** of receiving the request (Article 91 of the Criminal Procedure Law).

### 4. 逮捕 · Arrest

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第81条。

逮捕是经人民检察院批准或决定、或人民法院决定，由公安机关执行的最严厉的羁押性强制措施。条件是：**有证据证明有犯罪事实，可能判处徒刑以上刑罚**，且采取取保候审尚不足以防止下列社会危险性——可能实施新的犯罪；有危害国家安全、公共安全或社会秩序的现实危险；可能毁灭、伪造证据、干扰证人作证或串供；可能对被害人、举报人、控告人打击报复；企图自杀或逃跑。可能判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚，或可能判处徒刑以上刑罚且曾故意犯罪或身份不明的，应当逮捕。

**Legal basis**: Article 81 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Arrest is the most severe custodial compulsory measure, approved or decided by the people's procuratorate, or decided by the people's court, and executed by the public security authority. The conditions are: **evidence of criminal facts, possible sentence of imprisonment or heavier**, and release on guarantee being insufficient to prevent social danger — such as the possibility of committing new offences; a real danger to national security, public safety, or social order; the possibility of destroying or fabricating evidence, interfering with witnesses, or collusion; the possibility of retaliation against the victim, complainant, or accuser; or attempted suicide or flight. Arrest is mandatory where the person may be sentenced to 10 or more years of imprisonment, or to imprisonment or heavier and has a prior intentional crime or unknown identity.

### 5. 取保候审 · Release Pending Trial (on Guarantee)

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第67条。

取保候审是以保证人或保证金担保、责令犯罪嫌疑人、被告人**随传随到**、不限制人身自由的强制措施，但须遵守未经批准不得离开所居住的市、县、不得干扰证人作证、不得毁灭伪造证据或串供、在传讯时及时到案等义务。适用于：可能判处管制、拘役或独立适用附加刑的；可能判处有期徒刑以上刑罚但采取取保候审不致发生社会危险性的；患有严重疾病、生活不能自理、怀孕或正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女，不致发生社会危险性的；羁押期限届满、案件尚未办结、需要取保候审的。外国人同样可以申请取保候审（必要时可附加限制出境等条件）。

**Legal basis**: Article 67 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Release pending trial is a compulsory measure that does not restrict personal liberty but requires the suspect or defendant to appear when summoned, guaranteed by a guarantor or a deposit. The person must comply with duties such as not leaving the city or county of residence without approval, not interfering with witnesses, not destroying or fabricating evidence or colluding, and appearing promptly when summoned. It applies where the possible sentence is public surveillance, criminal detention, or an additional penalty alone; where a sentence of imprisonment or heavier is possible but release on guarantee creates no social danger; where the person is seriously ill, unable to care for themselves, pregnant, or breastfeeding and poses no social danger; or where the custody period has expired while the case remains pending. Foreigners may also apply for release pending trial (conditions such as an exit restriction may be attached where necessary).

### 6. 监视居住 · Residential Surveillance

**法律依据**：《刑事诉讼法》第74条。

监视居住是责令犯罪嫌疑人、被告人**在指定场所居住并接受监视**、不得擅自离开的强制措施，其限制程度介于取保候审与羁押之间。适用于符合逮捕条件但有下列情形之一的：患有严重疾病、生活不能自理；怀孕或正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女；系生活不能自理的人的唯一扶养人；因案件特殊情况或办案需要采取监视居住更适宜；羁押期限届满、案件尚未办结需监视居住。对符合取保候审条件但不能提出保证人、也不交纳保证金的，可以监视居住。

**Legal basis**: Article 74 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Residential surveillance orders the suspect or defendant to **reside at a designated place and remain under surveillance** and not to leave without approval; it sits between release on guarantee and custody in its degree of restriction. It applies where arrest conditions are met but the person is seriously ill and unable to care for themselves; is pregnant or breastfeeding; is the sole carer of a person unable to care for themselves; or where surveillance is more appropriate owing to special circumstances or investigatory needs; or where the custody period has expired while the case remains pending. It may also apply to someone who qualifies for release on guarantee but can offer neither a guarantor nor a deposit.

## 二、治安行政处罚 · II. Public Security Administrative Measures

对尚不构成犯罪、但违反治安管理的行为，公安机关依法进行调查并作出行政处罚。相关询问、传唤、拘留均有明确时限。

For conduct that violates public security administration but does not constitute a crime, the public security authority investigates and imposes administrative penalties. The relevant questioning, summons, and detention are all subject to fixed time limits.

### 7. 治安传唤与强制传唤 · Summons and Compulsory Summons (Public Security)

**法律依据**：《治安管理处罚法》（2025年修订，自2026年1月1日起施行）第96条。

需要传唤违反治安管理行为人接受调查的，经公安机关办案部门负责人批准，使用传唤证传唤；对现场发现的，人民警察经出示人民警察证可以口头传唤。对无正当理由不接受传唤或逃避传唤的，经批准可以**强制传唤**。公安机关应当将传唤的原因和依据告知被传唤人，并及时将传唤的原因和处所通知其家属。

**Legal basis**: Article 96 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law (as revised in 2025, effective 1 January 2026).

Where a person who has violated public security administration needs to be summoned for investigation, the public security authority summons the person with a summons certificate upon approval by the person in charge of the case-handling department; a person found at the scene may be summoned orally upon presentation of a police officer's certificate. A person who fails to appear or evades the summons without justification may be **compulsorily summoned** upon approval. The reason and basis of the summons must be told to the person, and the reason and place of the summons must be notified to the family in a timely manner.

### 8. 询问查证 · Questioning and Verification

**法律依据**：《治安管理处罚法》第97条。

传唤后应当及时询问查证。询问查证时间一般**不得超过8小时**；涉案人数众多、违反治安管理行为人身份不明的，**不得超过12小时**；情况复杂、依法可能适用行政拘留处罚的，**不得超过24小时**。在执法办案场所询问的应当**全程同步录音录像**，并保证被询问人的饮食和必要休息时间等正当需求。

**Legal basis**: Article 97 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

After a summons, questioning and verification must be conducted promptly. The questioning period generally **may not exceed 8 hours**; where the number of persons involved is large or the identity of the offender is unknown, it **may not exceed 12 hours**; where the circumstances are complex and administrative detention may apply, it **may not exceed 24 hours**. Questioning at a law-enforcement venue must be **audio- and video-recorded throughout**, and the person's food, necessary rest, and other legitimate needs must be guaranteed.

### 9. 行政拘留 · Administrative Detention

**法律依据**：《治安管理处罚法》第10条、第16条、第23条。

行政拘留是治安管理处罚中最重的处罚种类（第10条）。单个违法行为行政拘留**一般为1日以上15日以下**；有两种以上违法行为分别决定、合并执行的，**最长不超过20日**（第16条）。对已满14周岁不满16周岁的、已满16周岁不满18周岁且初次违反治安管理的、70周岁以上的、怀孕或哺乳自己不满1周岁婴儿的，应当给予行政拘留但**不执行**（情节严重、影响恶劣或一年内二次以上违反的除外，第23条）。对外国人可以**附加适用限期出境或者驱逐出境**（第10条）。

**Legal basis**: Articles 10, 16, and 23 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

Administrative detention is the most severe penalty in the public security punishment system (Article 10). For a single offence, it is generally **1 to 15 days**; where two or more offences are decided separately and executed jointly, it **may not exceed 20 days** (Article 16). Administrative detention must be imposed but **not executed** on persons aged 14 to under 16; aged 16 to under 18 who violate the law for the first time; aged 70 or over; or who are pregnant or breastfeeding an infant under one year old (unless the circumstances are serious, the impact is bad, or they offend two or more times within a year — Article 23). For foreigners, **an order to leave the country within a time limit or deportation may be applied additionally** (Article 10).

## 三、出入境与移民管理（针对外国人） · III. Entry-Exit and Immigration Measures (Foreigner-Specific)

外国人还可能因违反出入境管理法规（如非法入境、非法居留、非法就业、协助他人非法出入境等）面临专门的人身自由限制。

Foreigners may also face liberty restrictions specific to violations of entry-exit rules (such as illegal entry, illegal residence, illegal employment, or assisting others to enter or leave illegally).

### 10. 口岸盘问与继续盘问 · Questioning and Continued Questioning at the Border

**法律依据**：《出境入境管理法》（2012年通过，2013年7月1日起施行）第59条、第60条。

对涉嫌违反出境入境管理的人员可以当场盘问；对外国人有非法出境入境嫌疑、协助他人非法出入境嫌疑、非法居留/非法就业嫌疑，或有危害国家安全和利益、破坏社会公共秩序、从事其他违法犯罪活动嫌疑，经当场盘问或继续盘问后仍不能排除嫌疑、需要进一步调查的，可以依法**继续盘问**（并可在24小时内转入拘留审查）。

**Legal basis**: Articles 59 and 60 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law (adopted 2012, effective 1 July 2013).

A person suspected of violating the entry-exit rules may be questioned on the spot. A foreigner suspected of illegal entry or exit, of assisting others to enter or leave illegally, of illegal residence or employment, or of endangering national security and interests, disrupting public order, or other illegal or criminal activity, may — if suspicion cannot be dispelled after on-the-spot or continued questioning and further investigation is needed — be subject to **continued questioning** (and may be transferred to detention review within 24 hours).

### 11. 拘留审查 · Detention Review

**法律依据**：《出境入境管理法》第60条、第61条。

外国人有上述嫌疑，经当场盘问或继续盘问后仍不能排除嫌疑的，出入境管理部门可作出**拘留审查**决定，并应在24小时内进行询问。拘留审查期限**不得超过30日**；案情复杂的，经上一级公安机关或出入境边防检查机关批准，**可以延长至60日**；国籍、身份不明的，自查清国籍、身份之日起计算（第60条）。对患有严重疾病、怀孕或哺乳自己不满1周岁婴儿、未满16周岁或已满70周岁等不适用拘留审查的，可改处**限制活动范围**，期限**不得超过60日**（第61条）。

**Legal basis**: Articles 60 and 61 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law.

Where a foreigner's suspicion cannot be dispelled after on-the-spot or continued questioning, the entry-exit authority may decide to impose **detention review** and must conduct questioning within 24 hours. Detention review **may not exceed 30 days**; where the case is complex, it **may be extended to 60 days** with the approval of the public security authority at the next higher level or the exit-entry border inspection authority; where the nationality or identity is unknown, the period is counted from the date it is ascertained (Article 60). Persons to whom detention review does not apply — the seriously ill, those pregnant or breastfeeding an infant under one year old, those under 16 or aged 70 or over — may instead be subject to **restriction of their scope of activities** for **no more than 60 days** (Article 61).

### 12. 限期出境、遣送出境、驱逐出境 · Order to Leave, Repatriation, and Deportation

**法律依据**：《出境入境管理法》第62条、第81条；《刑法》第35条。

**遣送出境**（第62条）：被处限期出境未按期离境、有不准入境情形、非法居留或非法就业、违反法律法规需要遣送出境的，可被遣送出境，被遣送出境者自被遣送之日起**1至5年内不准入境**。**限期出境、驱逐出境**（第81条）：外国人违反本法规定情节严重、尚不构成犯罪的，公安部可处限期出境或驱逐出境，被驱逐出境者自被驱逐之日起**10年内不准入境**。此外，对**犯罪的外国人**，法院可依《刑法》第35条独立或附加适用**驱逐出境**（属刑罚性质）。

**Legal basis**: Articles 62 and 81 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law; Article 35 of the Criminal Law.

**Repatriation** (Article 62): a foreigner who fails to leave within a time limit, is subject to entry refusal, is illegally residing or employed, or must be repatriated for violating laws or regulations may be repatriated, and is barred from re-entry for **1 to 5 years** from the date of repatriation. **Order to leave and deportation** (Article 81): where a foreigner's violation is serious but does not constitute a crime, the Ministry of Public Security may impose an order to leave the country or deportation, and a deported person is barred from re-entry for **10 years** from the date of deportation. In addition, for a **foreigner who has committed a crime**, the court may impose **deportation** — as a penalty — independently or additionally under Article 35 of the Criminal Law.

## 四、其他执法情境 · IV. Other Law-Enforcement Contexts

除上述程序外，外国人在街头或口岸还可能遇到两种日常性的人身自由限制。

Apart from the procedures above, a foreigner may also encounter two everyday forms of liberty restriction on the street or at the port of entry.

### 13. 警察当街盘问（继续盘问/"留置"） · Police Street Questioning (Continued Questioning)

**法律依据**：《人民警察法》第9条。

为维护社会治安秩序，人民警察对有违法犯罪嫌疑的人员，经出示证件可以**当场盘问、检查**；对被指控有犯罪行为的、有现场作案嫌疑的、有作案嫌疑且身份不明的、携带物品可能是赃物的，可以带至公安机关**继续盘问**（俗称"留置"）。留置时间自带至公安机关之时起**不超过24小时**，特殊情况经县级以上公安机关批准**可延长至48小时**，并应留有盘问记录；批准继续盘问的应立即通知其家属或所在单位。经继续盘问认为需要拘留或采取其他强制措施的，应在上述期限内作出决定，否则应立即释放。

**Legal basis**: Article 9 of the People's Police Law.

To maintain public order, a police officer may, upon presentation of identification, **question and inspect on the spot** a person suspected of an offence or violation. A person accused of a crime, suspected of committing an offence at the scene, suspected of an offence with unknown identity, or carrying possibly stolen items may be taken to the public security authority for **continued questioning** (colloquially "detention for questioning"). The period **may not exceed 24 hours** from arrival at the public security authority, extendable to **48 hours** in special circumstances with the approval of a public security authority at or above the county level; a record must be kept, and the family or employer must be notified promptly. If detention or another measure is needed, the decision must be made within that period; otherwise the person must be released immediately.

### 14. 海关与边检的"小黑屋" · "Secondary Inspection" at Customs and Immigration

**法律依据**：《海关法》第6条第（四）项；《海关实施人身扣留规定》（海关总署令第144号）；《出境入境管理法》第59条、第60条。

"小黑屋"并非法律术语，是对口岸（机场、口岸联检区）**海关或出入境边防检查的二次检查／留置询问场所**的俗称。在口岸，边检可依法对涉嫌违反出入境管理的人员进行当场盘问、继续盘问乃至拘留审查（见第10、11节）；海关对**走私犯罪嫌疑人**，经直属海关关长或其授权的隶属海关关长批准，可以**扣留**，扣留时间**不超过24小时**，特殊情况**可延长至48小时**。被扣留人享有要求依法告知扣留理由、在法定期限内被讯问或释放等权利，孕妇、哺乳不满1周岁婴儿的妇女、已满70周岁老年人等应在4小时内查问完毕且不得送入扣留室。

**Legal basis**: Article 6(4) of the Customs Law; the Provisions on Personal Detention by Customs (GACC Order No. 144); Articles 59 and 60 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law.

The "little black room" is not a legal term — it is the popular name for the **secondary-inspection / detention-for-questioning areas** used by customs or the exit-entry border inspection authority at ports (airports and joint inspection zones). At the border, the immigration authority may conduct on-the-spot questioning, continued questioning, or detention review in accordance with law (see Sections 10–11). Customs may **detain** a person suspected of **smuggling**, with the approval of the director of the directly administered customs district or an authorized sub-district director, for **no more than 24 hours**, extendable to **48 hours** in special circumstances. A detained person has the right to be informed of the grounds, and to be questioned or released within the statutory period; pregnant women, women breastfeeding an infant under one year old, and persons aged 70 or over must be questioned within 4 hours and may not be placed in a detention room.

## 五、外国人的基本权利 · V. Basic Rights for Foreigners

在上述任何程序中，外国人与中国公民一样享有相应权利，主要包括：

In all of the procedures above, a foreigner enjoys the same rights as a Chinese citizen. The main ones include:

- **获得翻译的权利**：不通晓当地通用语言文字的诉讼参与人，人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当为其提供翻译（《刑事诉讼法》第9条）。
- **领事通知与探视**：被羁押或采取强制措施的外国人有权要求其国籍国驻华使领馆得到通知，并接受领事探视（《维也纳领事关系公约》第36条及中外双边领事条约）。
- **委托律师的权利**：自第一次被讯问或被采取强制措施之日起，有权委托辩护律师；委托律师是犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其近亲属的法定权利。
- **时限与家属通知**：各项措施均有明确时限，超过时限应立即解除；除法定例外外，采取拘留、逮捕等措施应及时通知家属。
- **申请变更强制措施／复议**：可依法申请取保候审、变更强制措施，对拘留审查、限制活动范围、遣送出境等不服的，可申请行政复议。

- **Right to an interpreter**: where a participant in proceedings does not understand the commonly used local language, the people's court, people's procuratorate, and public security authority must provide an interpreter (Article 9 of the Criminal Procedure Law).
- **Consular notification and access**: a detained foreigner, or one under compulsory measures, has the right to have the embassy or consulate of their country notified and to receive consular visits (Article 36 of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations and bilateral consular treaties).
- **Right to counsel**: from the first interrogation or the first compulsory measure, there is a right to retain a defense lawyer; this is a statutory right of the suspect, defendant, and their close relatives.
- **Time limits and family notification**: every measure is subject to fixed time limits and must be lifted once they expire; except in statutory exceptions, the family must be notified promptly when detention or arrest is imposed.
- **Applying to change the measure / for review**: a person may apply for release on guarantee or for a change of measure, and may apply for administrative review against detention review, restriction of scope of activities, or repatriation.

## 六、应对方案 · VI. What to Do

遇到人身自由受限时，保持冷静、依法主张权利，通常比对抗更有利。建议按以下顺序处理。

When your liberty is restricted, staying calm and asserting your rights within the law is usually more effective than confrontation. The following steps are recommended.

1. **先弄清"是谁、在查什么" / First clarify who is acting and what the measure is**：立即要求执法人员出示工作证件和相应法律文书（传唤证、拘留证、逮捕证、拘留审查决定书、扣留决定书等），确认办案单位、执法人员姓名或警号、措施性质与理由——尤其要分清是短暂、非羁押的"传唤／询问"，还是具有羁押性质的"拘留／逮捕／拘留审查／扣留"。

   Immediately ask the officers to show their identification and the relevant written decision (summons certificate, detention warrant, arrest warrant, detention-review decision, detention decision, etc.), and confirm the authority, the officer's name or badge number, and the nature and grounds of the measure — above all, distinguish a short, non-custodial "summons / questioning" from a custodial "detention / arrest / detention review / customs detention".

2. **要求翻译 / Ask for an interpreter**：如不通晓中文，明确要求提供翻译（《刑事诉讼法》第9条）；在获得翻译前，对关键问题谨慎作答。所有笔录应经翻译核对无误后再签字。

   If you do not understand Chinese, clearly ask for an interpreter (Article 9 of the Criminal Procedure Law); until one is provided, answer key questions cautiously. Every written record should be checked through the interpreter before you sign it.

3. **要求通知家属与领事 / Ask for family and consular notification**：依法行使家属通知权；外国人有权要求其国籍国驻华使领馆得到通知，并接受领事探视（《维也纳领事关系公约》第36条）。

   Exercise your right to family notification; a foreigner has the right to have the embassy or consulate of their country notified and to receive consular visits (Article 36 of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations).

4. **尽早委托律师 / Engage a lawyer as early as possible**：刑事程序中，自第一次讯问或第一次被采取强制措施之日起即可委托辩护律师，律师介入越早越有利。刑事拘留后至检察院作出批准或不批准逮捕决定前（最长约37天）是争取取保候审、无罪或罪轻处理的关键窗口（"黄金37天"）。

   In criminal proceedings you may retain a defense lawyer from the first interrogation or the first compulsory measure, and earlier involvement is more favorable. The period from criminal detention to the procuratorate's decision on whether to approve arrest (up to about 37 days) is the key window — the "golden 37 days" — for seeking release on guarantee, acquittal, or a lighter outcome.

5. **谨慎对待陈述 / Be careful about what you say**：中国法律不设"米兰达"式沉默权，但严禁刑讯逼供和以非法方法收集证据，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪（《刑事诉讼法》第52条）；对与本案无关的问题，有权拒绝回答（《刑事诉讼法》第120条）。应当如实陈述，避免虚假供述，也不要自我归罪。

   Chinese law has no Miranda-style right to silence, but it strictly prohibits torture and other illegal methods of collecting evidence, and no one may be compelled to incriminate themselves (Article 52 of the Criminal Procedure Law); you have the right to refuse to answer questions irrelevant to the case (Article 120). Answer truthfully, avoid false statements, and do not incriminate yourself.

6. **不签看不懂的文件 / Do not sign documents you do not understand**：对笔录和文书，先经翻译核对、确认与你的陈述一致后再签字；对记录有误处可要求更正或补记；对空白笔录应拒绝签字。

   Before signing any record or document, check it through an interpreter and confirm it matches what you said; ask for corrections or additions where the record is inaccurate; refuse to sign a blank record.

7. **自行固定关键信息 / Record key information yourself**：记住并尽量记录措施的开始与结束时间、地点、办案单位、执法证件号，保留（或依法要求复印）相关文书，以便事后核查时限是否超期、程序是否合法。

   Note and, where possible, record the start and end time of the measure, the place, the authority, and the officers' badge numbers, and keep (or lawfully request a copy of) the relevant documents, so you can later check whether time limits were exceeded or procedures were lawful.

8. **避免对抗与违法行为 / Avoid confrontation and unlawful conduct**：不要逃跑、不要销毁或伪造证据、不要串供、不要行贿、不要袭击执法人员——这些行为会加重处理后果，甚至单独构成新的犯罪。

   Do not flee, destroy or fabricate evidence, collude, offer bribes, or assault law-enforcement officers — such conduct aggravates your situation and may even constitute a separate crime.

9. **关注时限、及时主张 / Watch the time limits and assert them**：牢记各措施的法定时限（传唤／拘传≤12小时、特别重大复杂≤24小时；继续盘问≤24小时、可延长至48小时；海关扣留≤24小时、可延长至48小时；拘留审查≤30日、可延长至60日等），超期应立即要求解除或变更措施。

   Keep in mind the statutory limits for each measure (summons / compulsory summons ≤ 12 hours, or ≤ 24 hours where especially serious and complex; continued questioning ≤ 24 hours, extendable to 48 hours; customs detention ≤ 24 hours, extendable to 48 hours; detention review ≤ 30 days, extendable to 60 days), and immediately ask for the measure to be lifted or changed once a limit expires.

10. **善用领事保护 / Make use of consular protection**：尽快联系国籍国驻华使领馆的领保电话，请求领事保护与协助（探视、转递律师、了解案件进展等）。

    Contact your country's embassy or consulate in China and its consular protection line as soon as possible, and request consular protection and assistance (visits, referral to lawyers, information about the case, etc.).
